Process

Source. Verify.
De-risk. Deliver.

One playbook, run with discipline. The output is a site a developer can build on — with a dataroom a diligence team can close in days.

01 Source · Months 0 – 1·02 Verify · Months 1 – 2·03 De-risk · Months 2 – 10·04 Deliver · Months 10 – 14
Stage 01
Months 0 – 1

Source

We work a list. Most candidates die at the capacity screen — that’s the point.

Stage checklist
  • Zone scan against capacity data
  • Substation-level capacity screen
  • Jurisdiction and water pre-check
  • Title preliminary
  • Owner conversation
Outputs
  • Shortlist
  • Capacity screen
  • Term sheet
Stage 02
Months 1 – 2

Verify

A capacity study against the current ERCOT planning case. State filings made; position locked.

Stage checklist
  • POI-specific capacity study
  • Contingency verified
  • Texas filings on record
  • Security deposit set aside
  • Phase I environmental ordered
Outputs
  • Capacity report
  • State filings
  • PSA signed
Stage 03
Months 2 – 10

De-risk

Every gate, in parallel where possible, sequenced where required. Community, utility, and regulator engaged early.

Stage checklist
  • Zoning and permits
  • Interconnection study
  • Environmental complete
  • Wetlands and cultural surveys
  • County MOU and abatements
Outputs
  • Permit packet
  • Interconnection study
  • Community approval
Stage 04
Months 10 – 14

Deliver

A site ready to build. Interconnect secured. A dataroom that closes on schedule.

Stage checklist
  • Site plan approved
  • Interconnection agreement executed
  • All permits in hand
  • Construction-ready earthwork plan
  • Title insurance + ALTA survey
Outputs
  • Closing
  • Dataroom handoff
  • Construction kickoff
