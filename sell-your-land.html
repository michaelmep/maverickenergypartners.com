Does your land qualify?

You do not need to know the answer. We will tell you.

Most landowners have no way to know whether their property has real value to a data center developer, because that depends on grid capacity most people never look at. These are the signals that usually mean it is worth a conversation.

  • Your land is within a mile or two of a substation, switching station, or a 69 kV, 138 kV or 345 kV transmission line, even if you are not sure of the exact voltage.
  • You have been contacted before by a utility, a solar or wind developer, or another company about an easement or right-of-way on or near your property.
  • The parcel is 20 acres or larger. We also buy smaller parcels that sit inside a larger assemblage.
  • You are an heir, executor, or one of several co-owners of inherited or family land and want a direct buyer rather than a drawn-out listing.
  • You are a rancher or farmer open to selling all or part of a larger tract if the price and terms make sense.
  • Your land is currently listed and has not moved, or has been off-market and never tested with this type of buyer.
How it works

Four steps, most of the runway carried by us, not you.

01

You tell us about the land

Location, acreage, and anything you know about power lines or prior utility contact. Five minutes by email.

02

We screen it, free, no obligation

We check grid capacity, title, floodplain and zoning ourselves using public records. If it does not fit, we tell you within the week and say why.

03

No listing, no showings, no marketing period. Terms are usually a short paid option period followed by a cash closing.

04

Cash close

We handle title. Closing costs are negotiated up front. You are not carrying diligence risk; we are.

Why sell to a direct buyer

Frequently asked

What landowners usually ask us

How do I sell land near a power line or substation in Texas?

Start by telling us where the land is and what you know about nearby transmission infrastructure. You do not need to know the voltage or ownership. We do that research ourselves using ERCOT, utility and county records, then tell you honestly whether it is a fit and what it might be worth.

Do I need a realtor to sell my land to a data center developer?

No. We buy directly from landowners and brokers alike. If you already have a broker, we are glad to work with them. If you do not, you do not need to hire one to talk to us.

What makes land valuable for a data center site?

Proximity to a substation or transmission line is the headline, but the real driver is whether that substation has spare capacity to accept new load. Most nearby substations in Texas do not. Parcel size, shape, flood zone, water access and zoning also matter. We screen for all of it before making an offer.

How fast can you close?

Once we have confirmed the site fits, terms are usually a short paid option period of 60 to 180 days to finish utility and title diligence, followed by a cash closing. We move as fast as the utility and title work allow.

My land has multiple owners, or is in probate. Can you still buy it?

Yes. We regularly work with heirship property, estates in probate and land held across several family members. Tell us the situation up front and we will structure around it.

Is there a minimum acreage?

We typically look at parcels of 20 acres or more, but smaller tracts can matter if they sit next to a larger assemblage we are already evaluating. When in doubt, send it to us. The screen is free.

Do you pay cash, and is there a fee to talk to you?

Closings are cash. There is no cost or obligation to have us screen your land. Most of what we check is public record, and we will tell you plainly if it is not a fit.

Do you buy land outside Texas?

No. We work Texas only, across ERCOT and the Entergy Texas service area in the east. Knowing one state deeply is the whole point.
