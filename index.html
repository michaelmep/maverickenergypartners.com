What we screen for

Land near transmission is common. Land near transmission with capacity is rare.

Direct 345 kV access is the headline. The math behind it — whether the substation can actually accept new load today — is what separates a site from a story. We screen for it before we sign anything.

  • Substation-level capacity screen, current planning case
  • Contingency verified, not assumed
  • Title, zoning, water, fiber confirmed pre-LOI
  • State filings prepaid and on file

How we work

Three rules. No exceptions.

01

Capacity before land

We screen the grid first, the parcel second. If the substation can’t take the load, the dirt doesn’t matter.

02

Texas only

Texas has its own grids, rules, and posture. Working one state lets us know it from the inside, from ERCOT to Entergy Texas.

03

Filed correctly the first time

The 2025 Texas large-load law was written to filter speculative sites. We meet it on the first pass, every time.
