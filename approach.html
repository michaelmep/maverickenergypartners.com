Approach

We start with the grid, not the dirt.

Most land brokers find a parcel and ask whether it could be powered. We do the inverse — start with a substation that can take the load, then find the parcel near it.

How we work

Three filters before we sign anything.

01

Capacity verified

Every parcel is screened against the current ERCOT planning case. We confirm the substation can actually accept new load — in writing — before issuing a term sheet.

02

Texas only

Texas has its own grids, rules, and posture. We work one state deeply, ERCOT and Entergy Texas, and don’t pretend to know the others.

03

Filed clean

The 2025 Texas large-load law raised the bar on speculative filings. We meet it on the first pass, on every site.
